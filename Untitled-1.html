About Me

I’m Jerwin Cabaiz, a painter inspired by nature, emotion, and everyday moments. I practice master copy techniques in oil painting, studying classical artists to refine my understanding of light, form, and composition. Through each work, I aim to capture both the seen and the felt, turning emotion into art.
